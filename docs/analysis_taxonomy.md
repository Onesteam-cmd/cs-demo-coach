# Analysis Taxonomy

## 1. Aim
- underflick
- overflick
- slow target acquisition
- bad first bullet timing
- poor crosshair placement
- bad spray control
- bad spray transfer
- late shot after aim correction
- early shot before crosshair stabilization

## 2. Movement
- moving while shooting
- bad counter-strafe
- wide peek without advantage
- repeated peek into prepared angle
- poor reposition after contact
- exposed reload
- exposed utility usage

## 3. Duel Quality
- good duel lost mechanically
- bad duel won by aim
- unnecessary duel
- no trade possibility
- bad angle isolation
- multiple enemies exposed at once

## 4. Macro
- late rotation
- early rotation
- missed trade
- bad spacing
- bad retake timing
- wrong save/retake decision
- weak post-plant position
- bad information reaction

## 5. Utility
- useful flash
- useless flash
- team flash
- late smoke
- smoke gap
- bad molotov timing
- utility died unused
- execute without utility

## 6. Economy
- bad force
- bad save
- missing kit
- missing utility
- poor drop decision

## 7. Progress Tracking
- repeated mistake frequency
- mistake severity trend
- aim consistency trend
- opening duel quality
- clutch decision quality
- utility value trend
